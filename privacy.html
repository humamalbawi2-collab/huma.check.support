Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HUMA Check handles information when you use the iOS application.

1. Data collection

HUMA Check is designed to perform device diagnostic tests locally. The current version does not collect personal data for advertising, tracking, or sale, and does not transmit diagnostic reports to a developer-operated server.

2. Local storage

App language preferences and saved diagnostic reports may be stored locally on your device using iOS storage. This information remains on the device unless you choose to share a generated report using iOS sharing features or remove the app/data.

3. Device permissions and hardware access

Depending on the tests you choose to run, HUMA Check may request or use access to supported device features such as:

  • Camera and flash
  • Microphone and audio
  • Location for GPS-related testing
  • Bluetooth status/functionality
  • Motion and environmental sensors
  • Biometric capability information, where available through iOS

These permissions are used to perform the diagnostic function you request. HUMA Check does not use these permissions for advertising or cross-app tracking.

4. Microphone test

A microphone test may create a temporary local audio recording for the purpose of checking microphone input. The temporary test file is handled locally by the app and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

5. Sharing reports

If you choose to share or export a diagnostic report, iOS may present third-party apps or services selected by you. Information you share through another service is then subject to that service's privacy practices.

6. Children

HUMA Check is a device diagnostic utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Third parties and Apple

HUMA Check is an independent diagnostic application. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Apple Inc. Apple, iPhone, iOS, Face ID and other Apple marks belong to their respective owner.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact: humamalbawi2@gmail.com

If a future version adds server-based services, analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote push-notification infrastructure, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures should be updated before that version is released.
